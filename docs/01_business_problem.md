# Business Problem

## Background

The current expense approval process relies on emails, spreadsheets and manual communication between employees, managers and the finance team.

## Current Problems

- Employees may submit incomplete information
- Supporting receipts may be missing
- Approval requests can remain unanswered
- Employees cannot easily track request status
- Finance staff must manually copy information between files
- Management has limited visibility of expense trends
- There is no central audit trail of approval decisions

## Business Impact

The current process increases administrative work, creates a risk of data-entry errors and delays expense reimbursement. It also makes it difficult for management to monitor spending and identify outstanding requests.

## Proposed Solution

Develop a centralised and automated expense approval process that:

- Captures complete expense information through an online form
- Records submissions in a central dataset
- Routes requests to the correct approver
- Updates approval status automatically
- Notifies employees of approval decisions
- Maintains an audit trail
- Provides management information through a dashboard

## Expected Benefits

- Faster approval process
- Reduced manual data entry
- Improved data accuracy
- Better visibility of request status
- Clearer accountability
- Improved expense reporting
